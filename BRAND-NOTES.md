# Chi-Cafe — brand analysis & design direction

Working notes behind the concept. Written for the pitch, not for the public site.

## What the current site tells us

**Strengths worth keeping**
- A real founder story: Suzanne & Ludwig Jacob, the "Chi-Cafe-Erfinder".
- One genuinely great line: **"Regt an, nicht auf."** It is the whole brand promise in four words.
- A meaningful name: *Chi* = life energy. The packaging already carries the character 氣 — which literally pictures *steam rising over rice*. A coffee brand whose symbol is steam is a gift.
- Purpose beyond product: ~2 million plant-based school meals for children in Nepal.
- A warm, existing idea: every variant has a *character* ("Der Exotische", "Der Harmonische", "Der Sanfte").
- Trust signals: pharmacy availability, footnoted claims, loyal long-term customers.

**What holds it back**
- 7 typefaces on one page (Roboto, Poppins, Dancing Script, Noto Serif HK, Montserrat, Muli, Times) — the eye never rests.
- Many competing colours (brown, navy, sage, red buttons) and promotional blocks interrupt the calm.
- The story starts with *what* (ingredients, prices) instead of *why*.
- Product-first layout; very little breathing room.

## Start with Why (Simon Sinek's Golden Circle)

| Ring | Chi-Cafe |
|---|---|
| **Why** | We believe enjoyment should do you good. Nobody should have to choose between their coffee ritual and their wellbeing. |
| **How** | Full-bodied coffee, joined with plants from ancient Asian tradition — reishi, ginseng, guarana, pomegranate, acacia fibre. Gentle energy instead of a spike. No added sugar, gluten or lactose. And every tin shares: school meals in Nepal. |
| **What** | Instant functional coffee & mushroom blends, each with its own character — online and in pharmacies. |

The homepage follows this order exactly: **01 Warum → 02 Wie → 03 Was**, then purpose, voices, and an easy first step (the €3.75 sampler).

## Voice (copy principles)

Influenced by Caleb Ralston's approach to brand storytelling — clarity, specificity, one idea at a time, say it like a person:

1. **Clear before clever.** One idea per section, one sentence that carries it.
2. **Specific beats superlative.** "2 Mio. Mahlzeiten", "70 % Vitalpilze", "3,75 €" — not "amazing", "unique".
3. **Warm, calm, adult.** Formal *Sie* (matches the pharmacy audience), short sentences, no exclamation marks.
4. **Promise less, show more.** Health statements stay within the brand's existing, footnoted claims (EU Health Claims Regulation). Footnotes ¹–⁴ are carried over 1:1.
5. **Invite, don't push.** CTAs are gentle: "Sorten entdecken", "Jetzt atmen", "Probierpaket bestellen".

## Visual direction: "Stille Energie" (quiet energy)

- **Typography:** one serif, one sans.
  - *Fraunces* (light, soft optical axis) for headlines — warm, calm and premium; its italic quietly echoes the italic Chi-Cafe wordmark.
  - *Figtree* for text — friendly, highly legible, modern.
  - *Noto Serif TC* only for the 氣 glyph.
- **Colour:** rice paper, oat, sand, and the brand's own coffee brown (#5B3A29) — with sage and a reishi red used sparingly.
- **Space:** generous. Sections breathe. Max ~60 characters per line.
- **Imagery:** natural light, steam, hands, raw ingredients; product tins blended onto paper.
- **Motion:** slow, soft and rare. Respects "reduce motion".

## Surprises built into the concept

1. **Steam becomes 氣** — the page opens with steam condensing into the Chi character.
2. **Energy curve** — a calm, schematic chart: spike-and-dip vs. gentle wave.
3. **Chi-Charakter finder** — two taps to the right blend (brings back their own "character" idea).
4. **A breathing moment** — a 10-second guided breath in the middle of a coffee website.
5. **DE / EN** toggle.
