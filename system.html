Visual direction

Stille Energie.

Quiet energy. A calm, premium system built on one idea from the brand itself: Regt an, nicht auf — it energises, it never agitates. Every choice below follows from that.

Start with why

The homepage tells the story from the inside out: 01 Warum (the founders' belief) → 02 Wie (gentle energy, the ingredients) → 03 Was (the blends). Purpose, voices and an easy first step follow.

Voice

Clear before clever

One idea per section. One sentence that carries it.

„Jede Dose teilt.“

Specific, not superlative

Numbers and facts instead of “amazing” and “unique”.

„70 % Vitalpilze.“

Warm, calm, adult

Formal Sie, short sentences, no exclamation marks.

„Schenken Sie sich zehn Sekunden.“

Promise less, show more

Health statements stay within the brand's existing, footnoted claims.

„… wirkt sanfter und länger.²“

Motion

Slow

1.2s reveals, long eases. Nothing snaps.

Soft

Fades and gentle rises — like steam, never like a bounce.

Rare

Motion marks a moment; it is not decoration. "Reduce motion" is always respected.
